          How to use it

          Train 3 times a week, with a day off in between (for example Monday, Wednesday, Friday). On gym practice days, ask the coach whether to add it after practice or save it for a home day.

          Do every rep with good form. One clean pull-up beats five messy ones, and judges score the clean shape.

          Every 2 weeks, do a test on the Progress tab. The app moves him up a level when he's ready.

          The gymnast's shape

          Every bar exercise here uses a hollow body: legs squeezed together, toes pointed, belly tight, ribs pulled in. That's the same shape he needs on bars and rings, so strength and technique grow together.

          Stay safe

          Stop if he feels sharp pain in shoulders, elbows or wrists. Sore muscles the next day are normal; joint pain is not.

          Look after hands. Use chalk, keep calluses filed down, and skip bar work if he has a fresh rip.

          No added weights. For growing kids, bodyweight and perfect form are the right tools.

          Share the plan with his coach. They know his training load and can tell you if anything should change.
